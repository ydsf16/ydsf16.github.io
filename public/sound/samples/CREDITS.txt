v8-idle.wav — stable eight-cylinder idle, remixed into a seamless loop from
  "Eight-cylinder engine idling.wav" by Lumamorph, Freesound, CC BY 4.0:
  https://freesound.org/people/Lumamorph/sounds/636066/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: selected 2–6 seconds from the high-quality preview, mono conversion,
  55 Hz high-pass, normalization, and an overlap fade at the loop boundary.

The higher-speed recordings below are deliberately quiet texture beneath the
continuously synthesized engine. These are recorded Ferrari 458 Italia sounds.
They do not reproduce the exact engine state of the user's vehicle.

Tractor diesel sound pack — Kubota B2601 style simulation

tractor-diesel-idle.wav — close recorded tractor engine idle, from
  "tractor idle engine motor close1.flac" by kyles on Freesound, CC0:
  https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/637883/
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Changes: high-quality preview excerpt at 3–16 s, mono 24 kHz conversion,
  45–8000 Hz filtering, level adjustment, and 400 ms overlap loop fade.

The original recording does not identify the tractor's brand or engine model.
Kubota B2601 styling and 3-cylinder firing simulation follow the official
B2601 specifications (3-cylinder diesel, rated 2800 RPM):
  https://media.kubota.io/uploads/B2301-B2601-Tractor-Operator-Manual.pdf
This is a Kubota-inspired sound simulation, not an authentic B2601 recording
or a Kubota-sponsored product. The recording is CC0 and can be redistributed.
