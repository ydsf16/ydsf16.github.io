v8-idle.wav — stable eight-cylinder idle, remixed into a seamless loop from
  "Eight-cylinder engine idling.wav" by Lumamorph, Freesound, CC BY 4.0:
  https://freesound.org/people/Lumamorph/sounds/636066/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: selected 2–6 seconds from the high-quality preview, mono conversion,
  55 Hz high-pass, normalization, and an overlap fade at the loop boundary.

The higher-speed recordings below are deliberately quiet texture beneath the
continuously synthesized engine. These are recorded Ferrari 458 Italia sounds.
They do not reproduce the exact engine state of the user's vehicle.

Tractor diesel sound pack — single-cylinder

tractor-idle-v3.wav and tractor-rev-v3.wav — two layers from the same real
small tractor engine recording:
  "Tractor_Kleinland_Pony.wav" by aUREa, Freesound, CC BY 4.0:
  https://freesound.org/people/aUREa/sounds/268779/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  The source is a 9-horsepower small tractor, recorded at a tractor museum.
  Changes: took the low-speed section around 12.1–19.9 seconds and the
  accelerated section around 42.3–45.8 seconds from its public HQ preview;
  converted to mono 24 kHz PCM, aligned individual combustion cycles in the
  accelerated section to about 14.5 Hz, adjusted levels, and blended a 38 ms
  overlap at each loop boundary. The low-speed loop is about 7.8 seconds;
  the faster loop is about 3.5 seconds. Both are blended continuously by RPM.

The browser changes the playback rate with RPM and adds a quiet combustion
layer. The sound does not claim to reproduce any particular tractor model.

Engine illustration — generated from a reference photograph provided by the
site owner. The red fuel tank and blue-green one-cylinder crankcase are
drawn as a cartoon. The large rotating flywheel is an independent CSS layer.
The illustration does not identify any particular tractor model.
