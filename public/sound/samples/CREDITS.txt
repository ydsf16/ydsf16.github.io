The higher-speed recordings below are deliberately quiet texture beneath the
continuously synthesized engine. These are recorded Ferrari 458 Italia sounds.
They do not reproduce the exact engine state of the user's vehicle.

Tractor diesel sound pack — single-cylinder

tractor-single-cylinder-v2.wav — real single-cylinder tractor engine excerpt:
  "old Eicher tractor.wav" by viertelnachvier, Freesound, CC BY 4.0:
  https://freesound.org/people/viertelnachvier/sounds/157822/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  The original recording documents a 1957 Eicher ED 13/Ie (a), an air-cooled
  one-cylinder diesel tractor recorded in a barn. Changes: selected a stable
  20.34–21.87 second section from the high-quality preview, converted to mono
  24 kHz, filtered to 42–9000 Hz, adjusted gain, and matched a 25 ms loop
  overlap to avoid the perceptible level dip in the earlier 1.60 second loop.
  The resulting loop is 1.505 seconds. The recording is credited above.

The browser changes sample speed in proportion to engine rpm and adds one
combustion event per two crankshaft turns. The credited recording is from
an Eicher tractor; it does not claim to reproduce any particular model.

Engine illustration — generated from a reference photograph provided by the
site owner. The red fuel tank and blue-green one-cylinder crankcase are
drawn as a cartoon. The large rotating flywheel is an independent CSS layer.
The illustration does not identify any particular tractor model.
