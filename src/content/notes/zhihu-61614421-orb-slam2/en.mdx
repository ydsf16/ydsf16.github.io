Monocular SLAM initialization must recover relative camera motion and an initial sparse map from two views without known scale. ORB-SLAM2 evaluates both homography and fundamental-matrix hypotheses, selects the better explanation, then triangulates reliable map points.

Good initialization needs enough parallax, well-distributed matches, and a non-degenerate scene. Failure at this stage often appears later as unstable scale or poor tracking.
