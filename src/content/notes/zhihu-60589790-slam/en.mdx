Reliable ground truth is essential for evaluating SLAM trajectories. This note discusses a practical acquisition setup for recording reference motion and comparing estimated trajectories against it.

The key concerns are synchronization, coordinate-frame alignment, calibration, and clear error metrics. A good reference system makes debugging and quantitative algorithm comparison substantially more trustworthy.
