RANSAC estimates a model from repeatedly sampled minimal subsets, then chooses the hypothesis with the strongest inlier support. It is effective when correspondences contain a significant proportion of outliers.

Robust loss kernels play a complementary role in optimization by reducing the influence of large residuals. In visual geometry, a RANSAC front end plus robust nonlinear refinement is a durable combination.
