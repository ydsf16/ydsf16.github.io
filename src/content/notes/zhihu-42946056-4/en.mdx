This chapter summarizes the camera model used by visual SLAM: perspective projection, pixel and normalized coordinates, camera intrinsics, distortion, and the relationship between 3D points and image observations.

It also frames common estimation tasks such as pose recovery and triangulation. Accurate calibration and a clear convention for coordinate frames are prerequisites for reliable geometric computation.
