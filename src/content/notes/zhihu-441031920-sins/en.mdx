Initial alignment estimates the attitude needed before a strapdown inertial-navigation system can navigate accurately. Static alignment uses gravity and Earth rotation; dynamic alignment additionally uses motion and external observations.

Accuracy is limited by sensor bias, noise, latitude, observation duration, and vibration. A practical implementation needs explicit frame definitions, bias handling, and validation against known attitude or trajectory data.
