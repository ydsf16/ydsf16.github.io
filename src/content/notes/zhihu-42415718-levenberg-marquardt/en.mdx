Levenberg–Marquardt combines Gauss–Newton with gradient-descent behavior. It adds a damping term to the approximate Hessian, making early iterations more conservative when the local linear model is unreliable.

The damping coefficient is adapted from the improvement predicted by the model and the improvement observed in the objective. Large damping favors stability; small damping recovers fast Gauss–Newton convergence near the solution.
