EPnP represents each 3D point as a weighted combination of four control points. Camera-space control points are recovered from image constraints, then used to compute the camera pose efficiently.

Its computational cost grows linearly with correspondence count, making it attractive for real-time pose initialization. As with other PnP solvers, RANSAC and subsequent nonlinear refinement are important with noisy matches.
