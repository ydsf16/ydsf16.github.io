This is a personal overview of learning and working in SLAM, localization, and mapping. The field combines geometry, probability, optimization, sensor hardware, and engineering discipline.

Useful preparation includes strong linear algebra and programming fundamentals, regular implementation practice, careful evaluation on real data, and the ability to connect a paper's assumptions to an operational system.
