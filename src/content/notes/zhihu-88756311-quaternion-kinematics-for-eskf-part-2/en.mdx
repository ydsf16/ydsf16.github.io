This second note continues the error-state Kalman-filter treatment of quaternion attitude estimation. It focuses on the relation between nominal-state propagation, local orientation error, linearization, and covariance evolution.

The implementation must use one perturbation convention consistently, normalize quaternions where appropriate, and reset the error state correctly after applying a correction.
