Iterative Closest Point aligns two point clouds by alternating between correspondence assignment and rigid-transform estimation. The process minimizes a point-to-point or point-to-plane distance objective.

ICP is easy to understand but sensitive to initialization, outliers, partial overlap, and correspondence quality. Downsampling, normal estimation, rejection rules, and a coarse initial pose are often necessary in practice.
