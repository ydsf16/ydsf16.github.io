This note continues with C++ concurrency utilities, including condition variables, futures, asynchronous tasks, atomics, and common synchronization patterns.

The practical goal is predictable coordination: wait only for meaningful conditions, protect shared invariants, choose atomics only when their memory-order contract is understood, and prefer simple designs that are easy to test.
