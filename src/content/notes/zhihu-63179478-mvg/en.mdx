Triangulation reconstructs a 3D map point from matched observations in multiple camera views. Linear methods form a homogeneous system from projection equations; nonlinear methods refine the point by minimizing reprojection error.

Numerical stability depends on baseline, viewing geometry, calibration, and match quality. Low parallax and nearly parallel viewing rays make depth uncertain even when image matches look correct.
