This note collects the Lie-group and Lie-algebra identities used most often in SLAM: the exponential and logarithm maps for $SO(3)$ and $SE(3)$, the hat and vee operators, adjoint transforms, Jacobians, and first-order perturbations.

The purpose is practical reference. These expressions appear repeatedly in pose composition, uncertainty propagation, IMU integration, and nonlinear optimization. Keeping the frame convention and left/right perturbation convention consistent is more important than memorizing any individual formula.
