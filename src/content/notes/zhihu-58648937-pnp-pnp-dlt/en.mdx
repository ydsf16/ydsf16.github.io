The Direct Linear Transform (DLT) solves Perspective-n-Point by writing 3D-to-2D correspondences as a homogeneous linear system. After stacking sufficient correspondences, the camera projection matrix is recovered from the smallest singular vector and decomposed into pose.

DLT is conceptually simple and useful for initialization. In practice it needs normalized coordinates, enough well-distributed points, and nonlinear reprojection-error refinement for accurate pose estimates.
