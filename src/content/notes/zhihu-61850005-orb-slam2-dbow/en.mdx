Loop closure detects when the camera revisits a previously seen place, creating constraints that correct accumulated drift. ORB-SLAM2 uses a DBoW visual vocabulary to retrieve candidate keyframes efficiently from ORB descriptors.

Candidates are then geometrically verified before pose-graph optimization. Retrieval offers speed; geometric consistency prevents perceptual aliasing from becoming an incorrect loop.
