This article studies visual-wheel odometry built on the MSCKF framework. Wheel measurements provide motion constraints that complement vision, particularly where feature tracking becomes weak or the vehicle moves with limited visual parallax.

The central design task is to express wheel speed and nonholonomic constraints in the estimator state, account for the wheel-to-IMU extrinsics, and construct measurement Jacobians consistently. Calibration, timing, wheel slip, and the observability of scale and yaw all strongly affect practical performance.
