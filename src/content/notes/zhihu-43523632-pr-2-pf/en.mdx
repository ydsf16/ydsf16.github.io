Monte-Carlo localization represents the robot-pose posterior with weighted particles. A motion model propagates particles, a sensor model evaluates their likelihood, and resampling concentrates computation on plausible hypotheses.

The method naturally handles nonlinear, non-Gaussian, and multi-modal uncertainty. Its quality depends on a realistic motion model, a discriminative measurement model, enough particles, and resampling strategies that avoid particle impoverishment.
