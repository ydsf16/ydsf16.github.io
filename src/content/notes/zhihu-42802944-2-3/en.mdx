Chapter 3 introduces the mathematical tools behind rigid-body motion: coordinate transforms, rotation matrices, quaternions, Euler angles, Lie groups, Lie algebras, and the exponential map.

For SLAM implementation, the important lesson is to choose a rotation representation appropriate to the operation. Rotation matrices are convenient for composition, quaternions avoid singularities and are compact, and Lie-algebra increments provide a minimal local parameterization for optimization.
