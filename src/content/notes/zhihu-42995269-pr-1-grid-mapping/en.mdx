Occupancy-grid mapping divides an environment into cells and estimates the probability that each cell is occupied. Range measurements are converted into free-space and occupied-cell evidence, then fused over time.

Using log-odds makes updates simple and numerically stable: new evidence is additive, while prior and sensor-model assumptions remain explicit. The method is a practical foundation for robot navigation and localization.
