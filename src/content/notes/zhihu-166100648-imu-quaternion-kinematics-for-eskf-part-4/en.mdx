Using the approach in *Quaternion Kinematics for ESKF*, I implemented an IMU attitude estimator: the gyroscope performs propagation and the accelerometer provides updates.

## State

The state contains the IMU attitude from the IMU frame to ENU and the gyroscope bias:

$$
\mathbf \chi = [\begin{matrix} {}^G_I  \mathbf R & \mathbf b_g \end{matrix}]   \\
$$

## Propagation

The IMU kinematics from the book are:

$$
\begin{align*} {}^G_I \dot{\mathbf R} &= {}^G_I \mathbf R[\mathbf \omega_m - \mathbf b_g - \mathbf n_w]_\times \\ \dot{\mathbf b_g} &= \mathbf 0+ \mathbf n_g \end{align*} \\
$$

Define rotational error as:

$$
{}^G_I \mathbf R = {}^G_I \hat{\mathbf R} \text{Exp}(\delta \mathbf \theta)   \\
$$

The error-state kinematics are:

$$
\begin{align}  \dot{\delta \mathbf \theta} &= -[\mathbf \omega_m - \mathbf b_g]_\times \delta \mathbf \theta - \delta \mathbf b_g - \mathbf n_w  \\ \dot{\delta \mathbf b_g} &= \mathbf n_g  \end{align} \\
$$

With Euler integration:

$$
\begin{align*} \delta\mathbf \theta &\leftarrow \text{Exp}[(\mathbf \omega_m - \mathbf b_g)\Delta t]^T \delta \mathbf \theta - \delta b_g \Delta t +\mathbf \theta_i   \\  \delta \mathbf b_g &\leftarrow \delta \mathbf b_g +\bm\omega_i  \end{align*} \\
$$

In matrix form:

$$
\left[\begin{matrix} \delta \mathbf \theta \\ \delta \mathbf b_g \end{matrix}\right] = \underbrace{\left[\begin{matrix}  \text{Exp}[(\mathbf \omega_m - \mathbf b_g)\Delta t]^T & - \mathbf I \Delta t \\ \mathbf 0 & \mathbf I \end{matrix} \right]}_{\mathbf F_x} \left[\begin{matrix} \delta \mathbf \theta \\ \delta \mathbf b_g  \end{matrix}\right] +  \underbrace{ \left[ \begin{matrix} \mathbf I & \mathbf 0 \\ \mathbf 0  & \mathbf I \end{matrix} \right] }_{\mathbf F_i }\left[\begin{matrix} \mathbf \theta_i \\ \mathbf \omega_i \end{matrix}\right]         \\
$$

The covariance propagation is:

$$
\mathbf \Sigma_{k+1} = \mathbf F_x \mathbf \Sigma_k \mathbf F_x^T + \mathbf F_i \mathbf Q \mathbf F_i^T \\
$$

## Update

When the IMU is moving at constant velocity, the accelerometer measures gravity direction. In practice, departures from constant velocity are treated as noise, yielding the measurement equation:

$$
\mathbf a =- {}^G_I \mathbf R^T \mathbf g + \mathbf n_a  \\
$$

where

$$
\mathbf g = \left[\begin{matrix} 0 &0 & -1 \end{matrix} \right]^T
$$

is the gravity direction in ENU.

The Jacobian is:

$$
\mathbf H = \left[ \begin{matrix} [{}^G_I \mathbf R^T \mathbf g]_\times & \mathbf 0 \end{matrix} \right] \\
$$

## Initialization

As in the [previous article](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/152662055), use the accelerometer to initialize roll and pitch, and set yaw to zero.
