Strapdown inertial navigation updates attitude, velocity, and position directly from gyroscope and accelerometer measurements. The update must account for navigation-frame rotation, gravity, Earth rotation when required, and integration error.

This note organizes the propagation steps and their implementation concerns: coning and sculling corrections, coordinate-frame transforms, numerical integration, and error accumulation. These details determine whether a textbook equation becomes a usable navigation system.
