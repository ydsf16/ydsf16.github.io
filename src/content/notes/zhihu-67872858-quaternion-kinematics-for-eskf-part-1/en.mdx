This first note summarizes quaternion kinematics for an error-state Kalman filter. It establishes rotation representations, quaternion multiplication, the exponential map, and small-angle attitude errors.

The main idea is to maintain a nominal orientation while filtering a local error state. This keeps orientation on the rotation manifold and makes the linearized filter update well behaved.
