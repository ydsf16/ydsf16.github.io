ORB combines FAST keypoint detection with an oriented BRIEF descriptor. It is fast, rotation-aware, and suitable for the repeated matching operations required by real-time SLAM.

ORB-SLAM uses ORB features across tracking, mapping, relocalization, and loop closing. Scale pyramids, orientation estimation, descriptor matching, and spatial distribution are all part of obtaining stable correspondences.
