ORB-SLAM2 uses chi-squared tests to decide whether a reprojection residual is statistically compatible with its measurement uncertainty. Residuals that exceed the threshold are treated as outliers and removed from optimization.

The correct threshold depends on the residual dimension and confidence level. This test is a lightweight but important complement to robust optimization: it prevents grossly incorrect feature matches from corrupting tracking and mapping.
