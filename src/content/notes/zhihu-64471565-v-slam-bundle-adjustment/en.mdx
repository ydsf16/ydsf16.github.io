Bundle adjustment jointly refines camera poses and 3D map points by minimizing reprojection error across all observations. It is a core back-end optimization problem in visual SLAM.

Efficient implementations exploit sparsity and use the Schur complement to eliminate point variables. Robust losses, good initialization, correct Jacobians, and gauge handling are essential for stable results.
